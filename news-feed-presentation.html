Pull: 읽는 순간 친구들의 글을 모은다

Feed 요청→친구 ID 조회→posts 필터→정렬·20개
SELECT p.*
FROM posts p
JOIN follows f ON f.followee_id = p.user_id
WHERE f.follower_id = :viewer
  AND (p.created_at, p.id) < (:ts, :id)
ORDER BY p.created_at DESC, p.id DESC
LIMIT 20;
